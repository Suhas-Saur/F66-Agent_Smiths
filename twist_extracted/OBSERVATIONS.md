# Observation guide — frozen v6 rehearsal

Submit solution.py with decide(observation, state), returning action and state.
Preparation, feature engineering and decisions belong in that runnable pipeline.
You may compare algorithms and use any causal strategy consistent with the action
schema. Source examples and local checks are development aids;
only portal submissions reveal validation performance. Final results stay private
to organizers. See EVENT_RULES.md for limits, schedule and 20/30/50 phase weights.

Operate the parts desk using its tools and your own model gateway.
request_documents and catalog_documents retain document_id, entity_id, revision, status,
issued_at, received_at, lineage and text. Use current approved revisions as of the observation;
drafts and a late retry of an older revision are not new instructions. Delivery duplicates do
not create another part or request. Normalize mV/mW and connector aliases consistently. Missing
wattage is unknown, not zero; clarify, inspect and verify return permitted evidence and consume
the published shared tool budget. Read policy for current snapshot reliability. Choose how to
allocate calls, handle ambiguity and order requests; dispatch is irreversible. Keep factual
extraction separate from tool decisions where useful. You choose and pay for the model, HTTPS
gateway and infrastructure. The portal supplies no model account or provider fallback.
